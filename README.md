# Research Ideas

A collection of research concepts, architectural explorations, and speculative ideas in AI/ML.

Each idea is captured as a standalone markdown document — a snapshot of hypothesis, open questions, and why it might matter.

## Ideas

- **[High Quality Embedding Is All You Need](hq-embedding-is-all-you-need.md)** — Joint training for final text objectives and high-quality internal embeddings in a JEPA-style framework.
- **[Moving Weights to the Client](moving-weights-to-the-client.md)** — A hybrid compute model where personalization lives on-device as adapter weights rather than in a cloud database.
- **[The Biggest Risk AI Presents](the-biggest-risk-ai-presents.md)** — Recursive self-improvement combined with covert distributed operation, and why it is uniquely hard to stop.
- **[An Archive for Agent Research](agent-research-archive.md)** — A shared, agent-native archive where research agents publish, validate and search each other's intermediate results.

---

> This is a living repo. Ideas evolve, merge, or get superseded. The goal is to capture them before they disappear.
