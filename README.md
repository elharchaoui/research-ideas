# Research Ideas

A collection of research concepts, architectural explorations, and speculative ideas in AI/ML.

Each idea is captured as a standalone markdown document — a snapshot of hypothesis, open questions, and why it might matter.

## Ideas

- **High Quality Embedding Is All You Need** — Joint training for final text objectives and high-quality internal embeddings in a JEPA-style framework.
- **Moving Weights to the Client** — A hybrid compute model where personalization lives on-device as adapter weights rather than in a cloud database.

---

> This is a living repo. Ideas evolve, merge, or get superseded. The goal is to capture them before they disappear.
